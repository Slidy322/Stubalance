### **Step 2: Fill In Your Information**

- **Name**: Your full name (e.g., "Sarah Johnson")
- **Email**: Valid email address (e.g., "sarah@example.com")
- **Password**: At least 6 characters (e.g., "mypassword123")

✅ **Success!** You'll see:
- Alert: "Sign up successful! You are now logged in."
- Automatically redirected to Dashboard
- Status shows: 🟢 "Synced to cloud"

## 🎓 Common Questions

### **Q: Can I use the app without logging in?**
**A:** No, login is required to access any features. This ensures your data is saved and synced.

### **Q: Do I need to verify my email?**
**A:** Depends on Supabase settings. You may need to check your email for a verification link.

### **Q: Can I use multiple devices?**
**A:** Yes! Log in on any device and your data will sync automatically.

### **Q: What if I forget my password?**
**A:** Currently, contact support. (Password reset feature can be added later)

### **Q: Is my data safe?**
**A:** Yes! All data is encrypted and stored securely in Supabase cloud.

### **Q: Can I delete my account?**
**A:** Currently not implemented. Contact support for account deletion.
